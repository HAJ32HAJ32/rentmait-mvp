# Rentmait Contract Co-Pilot: Project Overview

## 🧠 The Problem
Renters in London often don't fully understand their rights because tenancy agreements are filled with complex legal jargon, unclear clauses, and landlord-biased terms. This leads to:
- Unfair treatment (unexpected rent hikes, ignored repairs)
- Financial losses (hidden fees, wrongful deposit deductions)
- Stress and anxiety over housing security
- Feeling powerless due to inaccessible legal knowledge

## 💡 The Value of Solving This
- **Empowerment**: Renters confidently assert their rights.
- **Financial Security**: Protection from hidden or unlawful charges.
- **Peace of Mind**: Clear understanding of legal standing without anxiety.

## ✨ Dream Outcome
> "Understand your rental rights instantly without legal jargon, expensive lawyers, or hours of confusing Google searches."

## 🔍 How Renters Solve This Today
- **Google searches**: Confusing, unreliable information.
- **Friends/Family**: Informal, non-expert advice.
- **Citizens Advice / Gov.uk**: Generic, hard to navigate resources.
- **Lawyers**: Expensive, rarely used due to cost.

These methods are slow, uncertain, and stressful—especially under time pressure.

## 🚀 Our Solution: Rentmait Contract Co-Pilot
- **Simple Upload**: Easily upload your tenancy agreement (PDF or DOC).
- **Ask Anything**: Freeform questions about your rights and obligations.
- **Instant AI Review**: Extracts relevant clauses (**break clause, start/end dates, notice period**) and provides plain-language answers.
- **UK Legal Context**: Built-in understanding of UK tenancy laws for accurate insights.
- **Supplementary Resources**: Additional contextual information from gov.uk quotes and summaries for supporting info.
- **Fast, Accessible, Private**: No legal knowledge required.

## ⚠️ Legal Disclaimer
All chatbot responses include a clear disclaimer:
> "_Responses provided are for informational purposes only and do not constitute legal advice._"
